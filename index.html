Privacy Policy: Dry Run References

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What this app is

Dry Run References is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to view pins from their own Pinterest boards as visual references for a personal creative project.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own boards and pins, read-only, through Pinterest's official API.

What it stores

Pin images and titles, saved privately on the owner's computer for reference. Nothing is sold, shared or published.

Other people

The app isn't offered to anyone else and collects no data from other Pinterest users.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest's settings and delete the saved references.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Pinterest account that owns this app.
